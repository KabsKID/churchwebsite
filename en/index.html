Who we are

  • One church, one faith, one Mission

    Welcome to the Authentic New Apostolic Church (ANAC). We are a Christian community dedicated to the pure teaching of the Word of God and the work of the Holy Spirit. Here, every soul finds a refuge to grow in faith and experience the true love of Christ.

    Our core values

    Authenticity

    A sincere and deep life of faith.

    Gospel

    Proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all with faithfulness and clarity.

    Communion

    Leading souls into a living and sincere relationship with God.

    Edification

    Fostering sanctification, unity and the spiritual maturity of believers.
